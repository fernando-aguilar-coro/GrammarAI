Hello there! Today, we are going to master the art of expressing wishes and regrets in English. When we talk about things that aren't real, or things we want to change, we use the words wish and if only. Remember, if only carries a much stronger emotional weight than wish.

Let’s start with present desires and annoyances. When you want to change a present situation, use the past simple tense. For example, you might say, I wish I spoke French fluently. Notice that even though we are talking about now, we use the past tense spoke. If you are complaining about an annoying habit, like someone else’s behavior or the weather, use would plus the base verb. For instance, I wish it would stop raining.

A quick pronunciation tip: wish is pronounced with a soft sh sound at the end, and only starts with a clear o sound. In natural, connected speech, if only I had often sounds like one smooth flow of words.

Next, let’s look at past regrets. When you feel sorry about something that already happened, you need to backshift even further into the past using the past perfect tense, which is had plus the past participle. So, instead of saying I wish I went, you should say, If only I had studied harder for the exam.

For future desires or abilities, we use could plus the base verb. If you want to go to an event but can't, you would say, I wish I could attend the conference tomorrow.

Before we finish, let’s clear up some common mistakes. First, always backshift your modals. Instead of saying I wish I can, always say I wish I could. Second, for past regrets, never use the simple past; always use the past perfect, like I had gone. Third, be careful with the word would. We only use would for behaviors and habits. If you are talking about a permanent state, like someone’s height, don't say I wish you would be taller. Instead, say I wish you were taller. Finally, remember that the verb following wish must always be backshifted. So, instead of she wishes she has, say she wishes she had more free time.

Keep practicing these structures, and you will find yourself sounding much more natural when expressing your hopes and regrets. You are doing a great job!
