Hello there! I am so happy to help you start your English journey today. Learning how to greet people and be polite is the very first step toward making great connections in English. Let’s look at four essential phrases that will make you sound natural and friendly right away.

First, we have the most common way to start a conversation: Hello. It is simple, clear, and works in almost any situation. When you say it, try to keep your voice bright and welcoming.

Next, let’s talk about politeness. When you are asking for something, always remember to add the word Please. For example, you could say, Could you pass the water, please? Adding that little word at the end makes your request sound much softer and more respectful.

Then, we have the most important word for showing appreciation: Thank you. You can use this whenever someone helps you or gives you something. If you want to sound a bit more casual, you can say Thanks, but Thank you is always a perfect, polite choice.

Finally, when someone says thank you to you, the standard response is You are welcome. In spoken English, we almost always use the contraction, so it sounds like You are welcome. A quick tip on pronunciation here: make sure to link the words together smoothly, like you are welcome, rather than saying each word separately.

To practice, imagine you are at a coffee shop. You might say, Hello, may I have a coffee, please? And when the barista hands it to you, you say, Thank you. If they say thank you back, you simply reply, You are welcome.

These four phrases are the building blocks of good manners in English. Practice saying them out loud today, and you will feel much more confident when you meet new people. You are doing a great job, and I look forward to our next lesson together!
