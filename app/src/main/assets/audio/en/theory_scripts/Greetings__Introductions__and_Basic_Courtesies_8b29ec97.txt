Hello there! I am so happy to help you master the art of English social interactions. Whether you are in a boardroom or at a coffee shop, knowing how to greet people and introduce yourself with confidence is the first step to great communication.

Let’s start with greetings. The tone you choose depends on the situation. In a formal or professional setting, you might hear the phrase, How do you do? Pronounced how-doo-you-doo. Remember, this is not actually a question about your health! The best way to respond is to simply say, How do you do? back, or say, I am pleased to meet you.

For a neutral or polite setting, a simple Good morning is perfect. You can follow that up by asking, How are you today? Now, if you are hanging out with friends, you might hear, What’s up? You can reply with something casual like, Not much, just working.

When it comes to introducing yourself, keep it simple. You can say, Let me introduce myself, my name is, followed by your name. If you are meeting a peer, a friendly, Hi, I’m, followed by your name, and, It’s nice to meet you, works every time. If you need to introduce two people to each other, try saying, John, I’d like to introduce you to Sarah.

Politeness is key in English. When you want to express gratitude, say, Thank you very much. To respond, you have options! Use, You’re welcome, or, My pleasure, in formal settings, and, No problem, when you are with friends. If you need to apologize, a polite, I do apologize for the delay, is very professional.

I want to share a few quick tips to help you avoid common mistakes. First, please remember that, How do you do, is a ritual, not a question. Don’t explain how you are feeling! Second, be careful with informal greetings like, What’s up, in the office. Save those for your friends and stick to, Good morning, or, Good afternoon, with your boss. 

Finally, when meeting someone new in a formal environment, always use their title, like Mr., Ms., or Dr., followed by their last name. It shows great respect. Once they invite you to use their first name, you are all set to switch to a more casual tone.

You are doing a fantastic job. Keep practicing these phrases, and you will sound more natural and confident every single day!
