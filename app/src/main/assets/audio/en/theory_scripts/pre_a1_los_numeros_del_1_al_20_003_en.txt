Hello there! Today, we are going to master counting from one to twenty in English. Learning these numbers is the perfect foundation for everything else you will do in the language, so let’s dive right in.

We will start with the first ten numbers. Listen closely and repeat after me: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, and ten. 

A quick pronunciation tip for the number three: make sure to place your tongue between your teeth to create that soft th sound. For the number eight, remember that the gh is silent, so it sounds just like the word ate.

Now, let’s move on to the numbers eleven through twenty. These can be a little trickier, so take your time: eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, and twenty.

Notice how the numbers thirteen through nineteen all end with the sound teen. When you say these, try to put a little extra stress on that second syllable, like thir-TEEN or fif-TEEN. This helps native speakers understand exactly which number you mean. 

Also, pay attention to twelve. It is a unique word, unlike the others in the sequence. And finally, for twenty, you can pronounce it as twen-tee, or if you want to sound a bit more casual, you might hear people drop the t sound in the middle, saying twen-ee. Both are perfectly acceptable.

Let’s quickly review those last few again: thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen, and twenty.

You are doing a fantastic job. Practice these out loud a few times today, perhaps while you are walking or waiting for your coffee. The more you say them, the more natural they will feel. Keep up the great work, and I will see you in the next lesson!
