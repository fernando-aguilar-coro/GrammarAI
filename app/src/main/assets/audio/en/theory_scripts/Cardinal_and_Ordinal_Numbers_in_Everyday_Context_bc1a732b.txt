Hello! Today, we are going to master the difference between cardinal and ordinal numbers. These are the building blocks of English, and getting them right will make your communication much more precise.

First, let’s talk about cardinal numbers. These indicate quantity and answer the question, How many? Think of them as your counting numbers. For example, you might say, I have one apple, there are two cars in the driveway, she is twenty years old, or it costs one hundred dollars. These are straightforward, but remember to practice your pronunciation to keep them clear.

Next, we have ordinal numbers. These indicate position, rank, or the order of things, and they answer the question, Which one? Most of the time, we form these by adding a th sound to the end of the cardinal number, but there are some important exceptions you need to memorize.

For the number one, the ordinal is first. For two, it is second. For three, it is third. From four onwards, it becomes more regular, like fourth. When you reach twenty, you change the y to an i-e-t-h to get twentieth.

A quick tip for spelling: when you have a compound number like twenty-one, only the final digit changes to the ordinal form. So, it becomes twenty-first, not twentieth-first.

Now, let’s look at some common mistakes to avoid. A frequent error is using a cardinal number when you should use an ordinal one. For example, when talking about a building, always say the fourth floor, not floor four. 

Also, watch out for the th sound at the end of words like fourth or twentieth. This is called a dental fricative. To make it, place the tip of your tongue lightly between your teeth and blow air out. If you say it like a t or an s, it can sound a bit unclear, so keep practicing that soft th sound.

Finally, remember that first, second, and third are irregular. Never add a th to these; just use the special forms. 

Keep practicing these in your daily life, like when you talk about dates or your place in a line. You are doing a great job, and with a little practice, these will become second nature to you!
