Hello there! Today, we are going to talk about some of the most common words you will use when ordering food or drinks in English. Learning these basic nouns is the perfect way to build your confidence in a cafe or at the grocery store.

Let’s start with our list. First, we have water. When you say water, try to keep the sound light and relaxed. It is a very common drink, and you will hear it everywhere. Next is milk. Notice how the letter L at the end feels soft against the roof of your mouth. Then we have bread. Remember, the EA in bread makes a short E sound, like in the word bed. After that, we have coffee. Make sure to emphasize the first syllable, coffee, and keep that double E sound nice and long at the end. Finally, we have apple. To say this correctly, start with a wide A sound, like in cat, and then finish with a quick pull sound for the L.

When you are using these words in a sentence, it is helpful to know if you are talking about one item or a group. For example, you might say, I would like a glass of water, or perhaps, I am buying a loaf of bread. If you are at a cafe, you could say, May I have a coffee, please? Or, if you are feeling hungry, you might say, I am eating an apple.

A quick tip for you: in English, we often use the word some when we are talking about food in general. So, instead of just saying bread, you can say, I would like some bread. It sounds very natural and polite.

Practice saying these five words out loud today: water, milk, bread, coffee, and apple. Try to use them in a full sentence, like I am drinking milk or I am eating an apple. You are doing a fantastic job, and the more you practice these simple sounds, the more natural your English will become. Keep up the great work, and I will see you in our next lesson!
