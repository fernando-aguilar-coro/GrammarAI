Hello and welcome back! Today, we are going to learn five common animal words that you will hear and use every single day in English. Mastering these basic words will boost your confidence in daily conversations. Let us dive right in!

Our first animal is dog. Make sure to pronounce that final "g" sound clearly at the back of your throat: dog. For example, you can say, "My neighbor has a very friendly dog." To make it plural, we simply add an S at the end, which gives us dogs.

Next, we have cat. Notice the short "a" sound in the middle, just like in the words "hat" or "apple," and finish with a crisp "t" sound: cat. For example, you can say, "The black cat is sleeping on the sofa." The plural form is cats.

Our third word is bird. Be careful with the middle vowel sound here. It is not an "i" sound, but rather an "er" sound, similar to the words "girl" or "turn": bird. Let us put it into a sentence: "Look at that beautiful bird in the tree." The plural form is birds.

Now, let us look at our fourth animal: fish. Make sure to end the word with a smooth "sh" sound: fish. Here is an important grammar tip for this word: the plural form of fish is usually just fish! So you can say, "I see one fish," or "I see five fish in the lake." You usually do not add an S unless you are talking about several different species of fish.

Finally, our fifth animal is horse. Remember to breathe out gently for that starting "H" sound: horse. Listen to this example sentence: "She loves to ride her horse on the weekends." When we make this word plural, we add -e-s, which adds a new syllable pronounced as "horses."

Let us do a quick practice together. Repeat each word out loud after me: dog... cat... bird... fish... and horse.

Wonderful job! You have learned five essential animal words, how to pronounce them correctly, and a key plural rule. Keep practicing these sentences out loud, and I will see you in our next lesson!
