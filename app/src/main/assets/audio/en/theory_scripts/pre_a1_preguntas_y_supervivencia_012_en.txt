Hello there! Welcome to your English lesson. Today, we are going to focus on three essential survival phrases that will help you navigate any conversation with confidence, even when you are just starting out.

The first phrase you should keep in your pocket is, What is this? You use this whenever you see an object and you want to know its name. For example, if you are at a market and see a fruit you do not recognize, you can simply point and ask, What is this? When you say this, try to let your voice go up slightly at the end of the sentence to show you are asking a question.

Sometimes, even when we listen carefully, the words might move a little too fast. That is perfectly normal! When you feel a bit lost, just say, I don't understand. This is a very polite and clear way to let the other person know that you need a little more help. Remember to pronounce the word understand with the stress on the second syllable, like this: un-der-STAND.

Finally, if you need someone to say something again, you can use the phrase, Repeat please. This is a great way to practice your listening skills. If you want to sound even more natural, you can say, Could you repeat that, please? 

Let us practice putting them together. If you are confused, you might say, I am sorry, I don't understand. Could you repeat that, please? 

Don't worry about making mistakes. Every time you use these phrases, you are getting one step closer to fluency. Keep practicing, stay curious, and I will see you in the next lesson!
