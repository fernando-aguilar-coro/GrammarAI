Hello there! Today we are going to master possessive pronouns. These are wonderful little words that help us show ownership without having to repeat the same nouns over and over.

The most important thing to remember is that possessive pronouns stand all on their own. Unlike possessive adjectives like my, your, or his, which must sit right in front of a noun, a possessive pronoun replaces the noun entirely.

Let’s walk through the forms together. For the first person singular, we use mine, pronounced like the word mine. You might say, This book is mine. For the second person, we use yours, pronounced with a z sound at the end, as in, Is this coat yours? For the third person masculine, we use his, which sounds just like the adjective, as in, That jacket is his. For the feminine, we add an s to get hers, pronounced with a soft z sound at the end, like, The red car is hers. For the first person plural, we use ours, and for the third person plural, we use theirs, with a clear th sound at the start.

Think of it this way: instead of saying, This is my car, but that is your car, you can simply say, This is my car, but that one is yours. It makes your English sound much more natural and less repetitive.

Now, let’s look at a few common pitfalls to avoid. First, never add an apostrophe to a possessive pronoun. Words like yours or hers are already possessive, so you never need an apostrophe. Writing yours with an apostrophe is a very common mistake, so be careful!

Second, remember that you should never place a noun directly after a possessive pronoun. You wouldn't say, This is mine pen. Instead, you should say, This pen is mine. The pronoun acts as the object, so it doesn't need a noun following it.

Finally, be careful not to confuse possessive adjectives with pronouns. And remember, the word its, when showing possession, never has an apostrophe. If you see an apostrophe in it’s, it is actually a contraction for it is or it has.

Keep practicing these, and you will be using them like a native speaker in no time. You are doing great!
