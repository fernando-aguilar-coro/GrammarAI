Hello everyone, and welcome back to your English practice! Today, we are going to master adjective and preposition collocations. 

In English, certain adjectives are naturally paired with specific prepositions. Because these are fixed combinations, translating them directly from your native language can often lead to tricky mistakes. So, the secret is to learn them together as single units!

These combinations usually appear right after linking verbs like to be or to feel, and they connect directly to a noun phrase or a gerund, which is an ing verb form.
